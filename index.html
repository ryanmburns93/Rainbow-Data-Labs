Discovery & strategy

We start by understanding your goals, data landscape, and constraints. Stakeholder interviews, data audits, and quick prototypes help separate high-value opportunities from noise. You leave with a prioritized roadmap and realistic estimates—not a one-size-fits-all playbook.

When the question is “should we use AI for this?” we answer with criteria you can reuse: impact, feasibility, risk, and total cost of ownership.

Implementation & integration. We design and build solutions that fit your existing tools—SQL warehouses, Python services, cloud platforms, and modern LLM APIs. Emphasis on testing, monitoring, and documentation so systems stay reliable after launch.
